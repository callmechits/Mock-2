About the website

How these mocks work

Signing in

There's no account to create, no email and no Google login.

  1. Open a live paper and press Start paper.
  2. Type your Reddit username (with or without u/). This is the name shown on the leaderboard.
  3. Choose a PIN of 4–8 digits and remember it. It's only for this paper.

Your PIN lets you continue on another device if your laptop dies mid-exam, and lets you see your result later from any phone or computer. Five wrong PINs lock the username for 15 minutes.

What to do

  • Use a laptop or desktop if you can, with a charger plugged in.
  • Close other tabs and apps. The exam runs in full screen, and leaving it or switching tabs is recorded.
  • Read the instructions, tick the box, and start inside the entry window — after that, new attempts can't begin.
  • Click Save & Next (or Save & Mark for Review) to save an answer, exactly like the real JEE Main. Just selecting an option doesn't save it.
  • Don't open the paper in two tabs — only one can be active at a time.

What to expect

  • Pattern: 75 questions in 3 hours — Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, 25 each (20 single-correct + 5 numerical). +4 for a correct answer, −1 for a wrong one, 0 if skipped.
  • Timer: runs on the server, not your device. The paper submits itself at 00:00 time left.
  • Bad internet? Keep going. Answers save on your device and sync when you're back online. Refreshing the page doesn't lose anything.
  • Results: scores, ranks, NTA-style percentiles and full solutions appear once the paper closes for everyone. Then you can also re-attempt it as unranked practice (I think I turned this off for now).
  • Your data: your username is stored encrypted and your PIN is never stored in readable form. After a paper is over, its personal data is deleted; only the leaderboard (username + scores) stays.
